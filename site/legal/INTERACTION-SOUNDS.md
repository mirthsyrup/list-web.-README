# Interaction sound sources — CC0 1.0

Metal, leather and cloth: Kenney, RPG Audio.
https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio

Wood and glass: Kenney, Impact Sounds.
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

Coins: StarNinjas, 12 Coin Sound Effects. Source recordings: coin.1.ogg,
coin.5.ogg, coin.9.ogg. Author describes recording coins rumbling in a hand.
https://opengameart.org/content/12-coin-sound-effects

All source works are identified by their publishers as CC0 1.0.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Modifications: level adjustment, brief fade and format conversion. Glass is
unchanged from the accepted v2 preview. Detailed source mapping and hashes:
interaction-sound-sources.json. Attribution is retained voluntarily.
